This Privacy Policy describes how FIRE ARENA collects, uses, and protects user information. FIRE ARENA is a Free Fire Max tournament and esports management application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and using your data responsibly.

App Purpose & Functionality

FIRE ARENA allows users to:

  • Register and log in using an email and password
  • Create a player profile for Free Fire Max tournaments
  • Join and manage tournament participation
  • View match schedules, results, rankings, and announcements
  • Receive in-app and push notifications related to tournaments

Information We Collect

To provide core app functionality, FIRE ARENA may collect the following user-provided information:

  • Email address (for account creation, login, and support)
  • Password (securely stored in encrypted form)
  • In-game name and Free Fire Max Player ID
  • Basic profile information required for tournament participation

How We Use This Information

Collected information is used strictly for:

  • User authentication and account management
  • Tournament registration and verification
  • Displaying match results, rankings, and user status
  • Sending important notifications and updates
  • Providing customer support when requested

Information We Do NOT Collect

FIRE ARENA does not collect or access:

  • Government-issued IDs
  • Personal contacts
  • Call logs or SMS data
  • Microphone or camera data
  • Precise real-time location
  • Background audio or screen recordings

Storage & Device Access

FIRE ARENA may request limited access to device storage for essential app operations, such as:

  • Caching app data for smoother performance
  • Storing temporary files related to tournament content

The app does not read or collect personal files such as photos, videos, or documents from your device.

Notifications

FIRE ARENA requests notification permission to:

  • Send tournament reminders
  • Notify users about match updates and results
  • Deliver important announcements and alerts

Notifications are used solely for app-related communication. Users can disable notifications at any time from device settings.

Internet Usage

The app uses an internet connection to:

  • Authenticate users
  • Sync tournament and profile data
  • Load real-time match information

All data transmission is performed using secure communication methods.

Advertisements & Third-Party Services

FIRE ARENA does not sell or rent user data to third parties.

The app does not perform cross-app tracking or profiling. Any third-party services used are limited to essential backend infrastructure (such as authentication or database services).

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Children’s Privacy

FIRE ARENA is intended for users aged 13 years and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If such information is discovered, it will be deleted promptly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be reflected by updating the Last Updated date at the top of this page.
